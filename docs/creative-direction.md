9 October 2026. **Historical route comparison.** On 12 October the user preferred route 3, Raw Bloom. The original recommendation below is retained as exploration history; it is superseded by that preference. Production tokens remain unapproved.

## The next decision

Choose the visual direction before developing production tokens, components, or the GitHub showcase. Compare three expressions of the same brand using the same existing copy. These route names are internal exploration labels, not new names or taglines for Capptus Edge.

The brief is grounded in the [supplied references](reference-notes.md). No external brand benchmark or new positioning research was needed for this first comparison.

## Recommendation: 01 / Electric Oasis

Develop the image the user liked into a more forceful agency expression: warm limestone, large heavy typography, citron, and unexpected digital cactus forms. Use scale, asymmetry, material contrast and image treatment to supply the rebellious character.

This route most directly preserves the supplied starting point. The credentials deck contributes direct Spanish language, growth-oriented content and digital nature. Its blue/yellow palette remains a comparison route rather than an additional set of mandatory colors.

The risk is drifting into tranquil hospitality or wellness imagery. Counter that with assertive headline scale, tightly edited content, surprising sculpture, and clear contrast. Avoid covering every application with decorative cactus imagery.
