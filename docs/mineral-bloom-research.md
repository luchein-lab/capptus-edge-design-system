## Observation

The six user-selected collages repeat cream paper, warm coral flowers and folded paper, sunlit rock, muted sea blue, and small yellow-green accents. Texture, shadows, torn edges and scale contribute to the mood alongside colour. They are generated conceptual landscapes, not documentary photographs.

## Design interpretation

Limestone and warm coral connect flat layouts to the imagery. Mineral ink gives text and structure strong contrast. Sun citron provides a limited bright accent. Signature blue remains a small recurring detail. The imagery carries most of the terracotta, olive and sea colours, so those hues do not all become interface tokens.

## Decision

The user selected Mineral Bloom for the company-wide repository baseline and explicitly requested replacing the previous GitHub system on 23 October 2026. The five published values are the selected designer-defined sRGB values, not exact image averages or values prescribed by the cited studios. The other three palette experiments are not part of the current system.

Blue's recognition as a standalone Capptus asset has not been measured. The earlier five-percent allocation was an exploratory heuristic; this system defines roles without requiring a universal coverage percentage. Print matching, audience associations and a full product accessibility audit remain separate validation work.
