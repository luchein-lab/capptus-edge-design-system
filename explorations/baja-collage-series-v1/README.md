# Raw Bloom / Baja Collages

Six additional Capptus Edge image assets inspired by the user's cactus/canyon/pink-paper reference. Generated with the built-in image tool on 12 October 2026. The screenshot was used as a style and material reference, not as an edit target.

1. Cardon & cove — botanical macro, quiet coastal bay, pink paper curl.
2. Dunes & bloom — sand, mountain horizon, oversized cactus blossom.
3. Tidal stone — mineral texture, coastal water and foam, paper rhythm.
4. Desert spines — cactus detail, eroded canyon and negative space.
5. Coastal horizon — wide desert/sea relationship, tactile botanical foreground.
6. Mineral arch — conceptual coastal geology, cactus and sea.

The shared visual language is physical photographic collage: torn limestone paper, terracotta rock, natural cactus texture, bloom-pink folded paper, and small citron accents. Muted blue sea extends the Baja/coastal subject matter without replacing the core palette. Natural lighting and photographic pigments do not imply exact color-token values.

All six images are text-free and logo-free. Original generated PNGs remain unchanged in images/. These are conceptual landscapes, not verified photographs of named locations. See prompts.json for the exact generation prompts and manifest.json for dimensions and SHA-256 hashes. The contact sheet is a review layout; use individual PNGs for design applications.
