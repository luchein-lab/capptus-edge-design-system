The palette and light connect the images: warm limestone, clay, bloom pink, natural cactus green, ink and selective citron. Human portraits are fictional adults and must not be presented as Capptus team members, customers or actual creators. Landscapes and installations are concepts, not documentary evidence of real projects or places.

Images intentionally contain no logo or typography. Add the verified original logo and real brand typography separately in layouts. Natural pigments and photographic light are not exact HEX swatches. The images remain creative proposals, not automatic approval of production brand rules.

prompts.json contains all ten exact prompts. manifest.json records the dimensions and SHA-256 of each original. contact-sheet.png provides a numbered overview; the full-resolution PNGs are in images/. No font files, credentials or external client material are included.
